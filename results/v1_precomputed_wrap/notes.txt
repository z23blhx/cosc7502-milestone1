Controlled change
-----------------
Precompute previous/next wrapped indices for every x and y coordinate once in
the Life constructor, then reuse them inside the existing nested neighbour
loop. The two-dimensional byte grid, two-buffer update, Life rule, traversal,
compiler flags, and benchmark interface remain unchanged.

Hypothesis
----------
Removing repeated wrapped coordinate/modulo calculations from the measured
96.61% V0 neighbour-calculation hotspot may reduce instruction overhead. This
does not claim a cache or memory-bandwidth improvement.
