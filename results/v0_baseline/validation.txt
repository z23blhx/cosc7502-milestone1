Required caveats
----------------
- The profile build used -O2 -g -pg rather than the -O3 benchmark build;
  profile time is diagnostic and must not be compared with benchmark time.
- gprof attribution may include inlined wrap work inside live_neighbours.
- No evidence yet isolates modulo, cache locality, branches, or memory bandwidth
  as independent causes. Those remain hypotheses for controlled experiments.
- V0 speedup is the reference 1.00x; no optimisation speedup is calculated yet.
