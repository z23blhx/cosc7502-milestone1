Controlled change
-----------------
Replace each separately allocated `vector<vector<uint8_t>>` grid with one
`vector<uint8_t>` in row-major order. Coordinates map to `y * width + x`, so
cells with adjacent x coordinates are adjacent in the allocation. V1's four
precomputed wrapped-coordinate arrays remain unchanged, as do the neighbour
loops, Life rule, double buffer, cell type, traversal, interface, and flags.

Hypothesis
----------
Contiguous row-major storage may reduce row indirection and make sequential
memory access more locality/compiler friendly. This is a hypothesis to measure,
not a claim that cache behaviour has already improved.

Formal Rangpur benchmark
-------------------------
Slurm Job 562443 completed 0:0 on a100-0. It used the same V0/V1 matrix and
resources: 512, 1024, and 2048 square grids; 800 generations; density 35%;
seed 12345; five repetitions; one node/task/CPU; GCC 8.5.0; and the same
`-O3 -DNDEBUG` benchmark flags. Every V2 live-cell count and checksum matched
both historical versions.

Median V2 runtimes were 1.063583010 s, 4.206093940 s, and 16.924774400 s.
Against V0, speedups were 5.360061x, 5.412149x, and 5.367999x. Against V1,
incremental speedups were 1.200263x, 1.205376x, and 1.202638x, corresponding
to runtime reductions of 16.684960%, 17.038348%, and 16.849437%.
V2 coefficients of variation were 0.421498%, 0.035717%, and 0.053105%.

Profiling
---------
Slurm Job 562444 completed 0:0 on a100-0 using the same V0/V1 profile workload
and `-O2 -g -pg` flags. V2 reproduced the historical live-cell count and
checksum. gprof attributed 82.38% (31.09 sampled seconds) to
`Life::live_neighbours` and 14.42% (5.44 sampled seconds) to `Life::step`.
The corresponding V1 self times were 40.96 s and 12.52 s. Profile-build wall
time fell from 92.7033395 s in V1 to 72.447904 s in V2. Profiling remains
diagnostic and is not substituted for the formal `-O3` results.

Decision
--------
Retain V2. The consistent formal 1.20x incremental speedup is compatible with
the hypothesis that contiguous row-major storage reduces row indirection and
improves contiguous access, but this experiment does not independently prove
the underlying cache mechanism.
