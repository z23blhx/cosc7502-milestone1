Controlled change
-----------------
Replace the generic 3x3 `dx`/`dy` loops and centre-skip branch in
`live_neighbours` with an explicit sum of the eight Moore neighbours. V2's
contiguous buffers, four wrapped-coordinate arrays, Life rule, double buffer,
cell type, traversal, interface, and compiler flags remain unchanged.

Hypothesis
----------
Removing repeated inner-loop control, the centre branch, and generic array
indexing may reduce instruction overhead in the dominant neighbour hotspot and
present a simpler fixed computation to the compiler. Improvement is measured,
not assumed.

Formal Rangpur benchmark
-------------------------
Slurm Job 562448 completed 0:0 on a100-0. It used the same V0/V1/V2 matrix and
resources: 512, 1024, and 2048 square grids; 800 generations; density 35%;
seed 12345; five repetitions; one node/task/CPU; GCC 8.5.0; and the same
`-O3 -DNDEBUG` flags. Every V3 live-cell count and checksum matched all three
historical versions.

Median V3 runtimes were 1.010598950 s, 4.002820110 s, and 16.074788300 s.
Against V0, speedups were 5.641080x, 5.686993x, and 5.651842x. Against V2,
incremental speedups were 1.052428x, 1.050783x, and 1.052877x, corresponding
to runtime reductions of 4.981657%, 4.832841%, and 5.022141%.
V3 coefficients of variation were 0.458922%, 0.114612%, and 0.087315%.

Profiling
---------
Slurm Job 562449 completed 0:0 on a100-0 using the matching diagnostic workload
and `-O2 -g -pg` flags. V3 reproduced the historical live-cell count and
checksum. gprof attributed 54.03% (8.24 sampled self seconds) to
`Life::live_neighbours` and 45.55% (6.95 s) to `Life::step`. V2 attributed
82.38% (31.09 s) and 14.42% (5.44 s) respectively. The profile-build wall time
fell from 72.447904 s to 47.1564199 s. Profiling remains diagnostic and does
not replace the formal `-O3` comparison.

Decision
--------
Retain V3. Its roughly 5% formal incremental improvement is consistent across
all sizes and exceeds run-to-run variability. This supports the fixed
eight-neighbour summation as a useful controlled optimisation.
